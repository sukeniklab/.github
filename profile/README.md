## Hi there 👋

We are the **[Sukenik Lab](https://sukeniklab.com/)** in the [Department of Chemistry](https://artsandsciences.syracuse.edu/chemistry/) at 
[Syracuse University](https://www.syracuse.edu). We study how proteins interact with their surrounding environment, in the test tube and in living cells.

The cellular environment is in constant flux. Cell volume, crowding, and solution chemistry change during the cell cycle, due to environmental changes, 
and as a result of disease. How proteins respond to these routine and pathological changes remains poorly understood. We address this gap with live-cell 
imaging, high-throughput screening, solution biophysics, and computational modeling.

### What we work on

**Disordered proteins as sensors and actuators.** Intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs) lack a fixed structure and exist as ensembles of interconverting 
conformations. This makes them sensitive to small changes in their physicochemical environment. We measure how cellular changes reshape IDR ensembles and 
how sequence encodes this sensitivity.

**Protecting proteomes from stress.** Many organisms survive dehydration, extreme temperature, and oxidative damage. We study the physical-chemical 
principles that let proteins tolerate these stresses, and the endogenous protectants found across all kingdoms of life.
